wedgie: an open-source DIY Ethereum hardware wallet

A wedgie is a pocket computer and hardware wallet you can build yourself, by Austin Griffith and Gus Griffith. Three off-the-shelf parts, no soldering: a Raspberry Pi Pico, a Waveshare Pico-LCD-1.3 screen hat, and a secure chip (Infineon OPTIGA Trust M) wedged in between.

A hardware signer for a Safe

A wedgie is a hardware signer for Ethereum. It signs for a Safe multisig with Safe's own audited contracts. The key is made inside the secure chip and never leaves it. Nothing signs until you press the green button, and the wedgie's own screen says what the transaction does (clear signing, ERC-7730).

Build one or buy one

Order the parts anywhere and print the case, or order a wedgie assembled or as a kit. Card or USDC. Hardware, case, firmware and this site are MIT licensed: build it, fork it, sell it.

More than a wallet

It runs MicroPython. Games, tools and apps install from wedgie.dev, and anyone can write one.

How

  1. For each descriptor, we render the screens for its test transactions, the way a wallet shows them.
  2. We pull what really happened from the chain: token moves, approvals, ETH.
  3. A blind reader (Sonnet) sees only the screen and says what it thinks will happen.
  4. A reviewer (Opus) reads the verified contract code, the screen, the receipts and the blind read, and lists what the screen gets wrong or leaves out.
  5. Code, not a model, turns that into scores: truth (is it right) and clarity (does a person get it).
  6. Every critical goes to two more agents, Claude and Codex, told to prove it wrong. Only problems both confirm are listed above.

We score the screen, not the contract. A contract that takes your tokens and says so on screen scores fine. Robot-written: the reviews are model output, checked twice, not an audit.
